AI Features · What SkoFy Does

Six ways the
AI works for you.

SkoFy's AI Engine runs silently in the background — finding the right match, verifying skills, checking schedules, and answering questions. You just get results.

🎯
Smart Matching — Not Just Category + Location
When you post a job, SkoFy's AI scores every nearby eligible provider on skill match, behavioral trust score, distance, and availability. You only see providers who are actually right for your job.
Distribution AI
🏅
AI-Verified Skills — Not Self-Reported
Every provider completes AI-generated assessments for their trade — specific to their field. An electrician is tested on wiring theory. A nurse on clinical protocols. You see an actual skill score, not just "5 years experience."
Skill Test AI
📋
Ranked Applicants With Full Reasoning
When providers apply to your job, SkoFy ranks them and explains why — skill score, job history, completion rate, any risk flags. You make an informed decision, not a guess based on a profile photo.
Hiring Agent AI
📅
No Double-Bookings — Ever
Before a provider can accept your job, SkoFy checks their existing schedule and real travel time using Haversine distance. If it's not feasible, they're blocked from accepting. Your provider shows up. Always.
Feasibility Engine
🧠
A Trust Score That Actually Means Something
The HCI score measures 10 real behavioral dimensions — not just star ratings. Trust, reliability, skill, emotional IQ, consistency. It updates after every job. The more a provider works, the more accurately it reflects who they are.
HCI Engine
💬
Support That Actually Knows Your Case
When you need help, SkoFy's AI support already knows your job history, payments, and what's happening in your booking. It answers immediately and accurately — escalating to a human only when truly necessary.
Support AI

For Customers
You see an actual multi-dimensional score — not just stars. You know if a provider is improving or declining, how reliable they are, and if there are any risk flags. Before you hire, not after.
  • See exactly why a provider is recommended — skill score, distance, rating, reliability
  • Risk flags shown before hire — high no-show rate, low completion rate highlighted upfront
  • Know whether a provider is improving or slipping — live trajectory shown
  • Choose your priority — fastest match, best quality, or give new providers a fair chance
For Providers
Your HCI score is portable and permanent — it belongs to you, not to the platform. Do great work, and your score proves it to every future customer. New providers get a fair start from Day 1.
  • Your reputation follows you — across jobs, cities, and verticals
  • New providers get fair exposure — HCI's fairness boost ensures new workers see real jobs
  • Specific, actionable feedback — the system tells you exactly what to improve
  • Skill test certificates — AI-verified credentials that customers can trust
