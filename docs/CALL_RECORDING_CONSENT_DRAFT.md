# Call recording: consent and privacy wording (DRAFT, for legal review)

**Status:** a draft for Dodorez's lawyer. It isn't legal advice and isn't approved.
Recording stays switched off (`CALL_RECORDING_ENABLED=false`) until this is
approved and added to the app and the privacy policy.

**Why it matters:** several US states, including California, Florida,
Illinois, Washington, Pennsylvania, Massachusetts, Maryland, Montana and New
Hampshire, require everyone on a call to agree before it's recorded. The design
gives notice on every call, before it's answered, in both apps.

## 1. In the call screen (both apps)

Shown while the call rings, on both the caller's and the callee's screen, and
throughout the call:

> 🔴 **This call is recorded for quality and safety**

**Proposed addition:** a short spoken notice that both people hear when the call
connects. Suggested text: *"This call is recorded by Dodorez for quality and
safety."* It isn't built yet; the lawyer should say whether it's needed in
addition to the on-screen notice.

## 2. When signing up, and in the terms (both apps)

> **Calls in Dodorez are recorded.** Voice calls between customers and
> providers made in the Dodorez app are recorded and stored securely by
> Dodorez. By making or answering a call in the app, you agree to it being
> recorded. If you don't want a call recorded, use the in-app chat instead.

## 3. Privacy policy section

> **Recorded calls**
>
> **What we record:** the audio of voice calls between customers and providers
> made in the Dodorez app, with the time, length and the people on the call.
>
> **Why:** to keep people safe, to resolve disputes and complaints, and to
> check and improve the quality of our service. We don't use call recordings
> to train artificial intelligence, and we don't sell them.
>
> **Who can listen:** only Dodorez staff who handle safety, disputes and
> support, and only when needed for those purposes. Every time a recording is
> played, we log who played it and when.
>
> **Where they're kept:** on servers Dodorez operates, encrypted, and never
> shared with the other party or with advertisers.
>
> **How long:** we delete recordings after 90 days. If the job has an open
> dispute or support request, we keep its recordings until that's resolved,
> and then delete them.
>
> **Your choices:** you can use the in-app chat instead of calling. You can
> ask for a copy of, or the deletion of, recordings of your calls by
> contacting support, unless we must keep a recording for an open dispute or
> by law.

## Questions for the lawyer

3. Is 90 days (longer while a dispute or ticket is open) appropriate?
