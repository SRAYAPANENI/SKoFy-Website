### Summary

Of the 6 spec'd components (updated 2026-09-12): **2 fully/near-fully built** (Skill Test Agent — exceeded spec; HCI Engine — real 8-dimension engine with confidence+trajectory, corrected from a prior stale "stub" entry in this doc), **2 partially built** (Distribution Agent — core matching + fairness boost work, auto-expand still doesn't; Hiring Agent — ranking is real and now includes the fairness boost too, but still a reused formula rather than the spec's full 8-signal one, and no mode selector/risk flags), **2 not built** (Feasibility Engine, Support AI).

## Pickup & Drop review (2026-10-14)

Built, tested and deployed 2026-10-14 (server: migration 050); the apps need a rebuild:
- **No alcohol.** `liquor_store` is removed from `PICKUP_ALLOWED_PLACE_TYPES`; the US needs a licence and a 21+ ID check. The customer form lists what can't be delivered.
- **Proof of delivery.** ROUTE jobs gain the `HANDBACK_CODE` step (policy). `app/services/delivery_service.py` issues the customer's drop-off code, never texted, and checks it with the provider's GPS at the drop-off (500 m, 3 attempts). A provider can only complete after it (`JobFlow.require_done`); the customer confirming receipt is still enough on their side.
- **Delivery details.** Migration 050 adds `dropoff_address`, `dropoff_notes`, `pickup_purchase` (PREPAID / BUY_FOR_ME), `purchase_limit` and `delivered_at`. The rules live in `app/domain/pickup.py` (pure); the invoice can't put goods over a BUY_FOR_ME limit.
- **Tracking.** The customer's map and ETA point at the drop-off once the order is collected (`tracking_destination`, `app/domain/geo.py`). The provider's Navigate goes to the store, then to the drop-off.
- **Wording.** "Your order is collected — review and pay" instead of "on the way" before payment, and "Delivery fee" instead of "Visiting fee".
- **Timing.** "As soon as possible" (HIGH) or a scheduled time (LOW + `scheduled_at`) instead of always MEDIUM.

**Buy it for me money hold, and what to collect (2026-10-15, migration 051; written and tested, NOT yet deployed).**
- No 12% on goods. The customer pays a **shopping fee**: 5% of what was bought, at least $1.99 and at most $9.99 (`shopping_fee` in `app/domain/pickup.py`).
- **Hold.** After the delivery fee, the customer holds the limit plus the largest possible fee, plus the card fee. This is payment purpose `GOODS_ADVANCE`, created at `POST /payments/goods-hold-intent`.
- **Pickup.** The provider can't confirm pickup until the money is held, and has to enter the receipt total (`goods_amount`), which can't be more than the limit.
- **Split.** When the job ends (`Settlement.SPLIT`), the receipt total goes to the provider, the fee to Dodorez, and the rest back to the customer. Split goods don't count in the provider's earnings.
- **Disputes and cancelling.** If a dispute goes the customer's way, the whole hold is refunded. A provider who has bought the items can't cancel. Their invoice is only the delivery charge.
- **Order details.** `jobs.pickup_details` holds the order name, order number, store pickup code and store contact. Only the customer and the hired provider see them, and the code is hidden once the order is collected. The customer can send or change the code with `PATCH /jobs/{id}/pickup-code`.
- **Shopping list.** `jobs.shopping_list` holds the item, quantity and what to do if it's out of stock.
- **Apps.** The customer app has the form editors, the hold card and the pickup code card. The provider app has an "At the store" card (order details, or a shopping checklist), the receipt total input, and a delivery-charge-only invoice.
- **No-show sweep honours booked times** (`app/domain/no_show.py`): the 15/45-minute grace starts at `scheduled_at` when that's later than the hire. Before this, a job booked for later was cancelled 45 minutes after hiring.

## Pickup from a person (2026-10-21)

Pickup & Drop can now collect from **any address** (a friend's house), not just a store. Migration 062; rules in `app/domain/pickup.py`.

- **Customer.** "From a store / From a person" toggle. A person pickup needs the item, its value (up to `PERSONAL_PICKUP_MAX_VALUE`, default $500), who hands it over, and a declaration that it isn't on the banned list (`PERSON_PICKUP_BANNED`: alcohol, tobacco/vapes, cannabis, medication, weapons, cash, anything illegal). Buy-for-me isn't allowed. The customer needs an ID **on file** (`customer_repo.has_id_on_file`) — not yet reviewed; real review comes with the KYC vendor.
- **Provider.** A "Pickup from a person" card: three checks (sees it, it matches, it isn't banned) gate Confirm Pickup (`item_checked`, stored in the step evidence). Or "Can't take it" (`POST /jobs/{id}/pickup/refuse`, at the pickup only): the job is cancelled, the trip is still paid (`visit_done`), the customer is pushed and the chat shows the reason. "Nobody there" needs a photo of the door (`REFUSAL_NEEDS_PHOTO`), kept in the step evidence.
- **Cancel cause** (migration 063, `app/domain/cancellation.py`). Every cancel records who caused it: CUSTOMER, PROVIDER, NO_SHOW, PICKUP_REFUSED or INVOICE_REJECTED. Provider reliability (HCI) counts only PROVIDER and NO_SHOW (`provider_fault_cancel_sql`). Before this it counted every cancelled job they were hired on, including the customer calling it off. Old cancels are backfilled where the reason shows the cause; the rest stay NULL and don't count. Still not counted: a provider backing out when the job reopens to other applicants (the job isn't cancelled, so it has no cause).
- **Finding the address.** Besides Google search: the customer's saved addresses as one-tap chips, "save this address for next time", and a pasted location link (WhatsApp / Google Maps / Apple Maps). `POST /jobs/pickup-location-link` reads the pin, or the place's name to search for (`app/domain/location_link.py`); short links are followed hop by hop through map hosts only (`LocationLinkService`). In store mode an empty search offers to switch to "From a person".
- **Local trips only.** The pickup must be within `PICKUP_MAX_TRIP_KM` (80 km ≈ 50 miles, straight-line) of the drop-off (`validate_trip`). Providers are matched near the pickup, so a pickup in another city reached nobody: a live test job had its pickup 283 km from the drop-off and its only provider. The app shows the trip distance, blocks a too-far pickup, and after posting says when no provider is near the pickup yet.
- **Bring it to me / Send from me.** From a person, the customer picks the direction: their location is the drop-off, or the pickup (the searched place becomes the drop-off, and the form asks who receives it: `recipient_name` / `recipient_phone` in `pickup_details`). Sent from the customer, tracking shows "Drop-off code for {recipient}" with a Share button, since the recipient gives the code to the provider.
- **Pickup address** (migration 064, `jobs.pickup_address`): saved as the customer chose it, the pair of `dropoff_address`. The customer's `full_address` falls back to it. A provider sees a person's addresses (pickup and drop-off) only once hired (`route_addresses_for`); a store's address shows before applying.
- **Rules endpoint.** `GET /jobs/pickup-from-person` gives the apps the cap, the banned list and the refusal reasons.
- **Admin portal.** The job view shows the item, its value, the declaration, the hand-over contact and any refusal; the timeline names who caused a cancel from `cancel_cause`, and a refusal step shows its reason and door photo.
- **Open.** Needs the lawyer's review: see [LEGAL_QUESTIONS.md](LEGAL_QUESTIONS.md) §2.


## Money rules (2026-10-21)

Built and deployed from [MONEY_RULES_DESIGN.md](MONEY_RULES_DESIGN.md) (migrations 065–069). Pure rules in `app/domain/` (`fees`, `cancellation_terms`, `reliability`, `inspection_proof`, `strikes`, `milestones`, `settlement`); services `FeeService`, `CancellationPolicy`, `InspectionService`, `DisputeService`, `StrikeService`; routers `fees`, `disputes`, `strikes`.

- **Fair Cap.** The provider fee is worked out at payout (`FeeService.settle_payout_fee`, locked per provider) and recorded per payment (`provider_fee_amount`, `fee_month`). The customer pays 3% capped at $15 instead of the card surcharge. Offers are capped at the fees a payment carries. Provider app: "You'll keep $X" on quotes; month against the cap on Earnings. Admin: fixed plans on the provider panel.
- **$15 minimum visiting fee** when the provider travels (set at hire, validated on rates).
- **Cancellations by stage.** Free before set-off or within 5 minutes; half the visiting fee after set-off; all of it after "I'm here" or "Customer not here" (15-minute wait). A late provider: free cancel, $10 credit, a lapse. Cancellation money is fee-free (`Settlement.SHARE`). 3 lapses in 30 days pause a provider (`providers.paused_until`, `takes_new_jobs_sql`). Customer app shows the split before confirming.
- **Inspection proof.** A report (findings, camera photos, result) replaces one-tap "done"; the customer confirms in the app, by an end code, or by accepting the quote. Otherwise every check passing pays after 24 hours; a flag holds it for support. Older apps' one-tap "done" is held for review.
- **Disputes and strikes.** "Inspection wasn't done" holds the fee; both sides add statements; support decides outcome and finding in the portal's Inspection Checks (and on job disputes). A contradicted claim is a 12-month strike with an appeal to a senior agent; consequences are off until the terms describe them.
- **Paying outside the app (§10, migration 071).** Chat messages are screened (`app/domain/off_app.py`). Phone numbers and emails are hidden from the other side; payment-app, cash and "outside the app" talk is delivered with a warning under it. Both create a report. Every customer rating screen asks "Did they ask you to pay outside the app?". Support decides each case in Portal → Off-app Payments, as a strike or no action (`OffAppService`). `customer_strikes` became `strikes` (`role`, `reason`). A provider's second off-app strike pauses them for 30 days, but only with consequences on. Provider app: Settings → Account standing.
- **Less pain on a job (§11, migration 072).** The visiting fee counts toward an accepted quote. Parts can be paid when work starts on any quote (up to the listed parts' cost). Added work mid-job (`ExtraWorkService`). Tips with no fees (`TipService`). A typical price range from accepted quotes nearby (`PriceGuideService`). Cash App Pay and pay-over-time available behind `EXTRA_PAYMENT_METHODS`.
- **Promises that cost Dodorez (§12, migration 073).** The guarantee (`GuaranteeService`; chat reopens for a fix via `contact_gate`). Lost chargebacks (`ChargebackService`; recoveries taken from payouts). Dispute deadlines (an hourly task decides small or overdue visiting fees on the evidence and marks overdue job disputes). Payout arrival dates on earnings. The reserve view. Portal → Guarantee & Chargebacks.
- **Repeat bookings (§13, migration 074, JOB_SHAPE_DESIGN phase 5).** `SeriesService` and the hourly `create_series_visits` task. Visits are direct requests; the visiting fee and quotes within the limit are paid from the saved card (`PaymentService.pay_with_saved_card`). Apps: Book regularly (Job history), Regular bookings / Regular customers.
- **Bookings paid up front (§14, migration 075, JOB_SHAPE_DESIGN phase 4).** The full price is held at booking (`UpfrontBookingService`) and released on completion; cancellation and missed drop-off shares are paid as `JOB_COST` shares (`UpfrontShares`).
