Questions

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining, applying and getting matched are free. A fee is taken only from jobs you’re paid for, and it’s capped. See the table above.

Do I need a licence?

Not for most jobs here: home help, everyday tasks and online work don’t need one. Trades that need a state licence (like electrical or plumbing) will ask you to upload it.

How much can I earn?

You set your own price for each job, and you choose how many jobs you take. Tips are 100% yours.

How do I get paid?

Customers pay through the app. Once a job is done, your payout goes to your bank account, and the app shows the date it arrives.

Can I work online from anywhere?

Yes. Online jobs like tutoring, design, bookkeeping and virtual assistance can be done from anywhere in the US.

I run a business with staff. Can we join?

Yes. Businesses can join as a team, take jobs and assign them to their members.
