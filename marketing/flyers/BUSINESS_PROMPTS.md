# "Got a crew? We've got the customers." — character prompts

For `business.html` (Dodorez for businesses). The story runs left to right in
three beats: **Sara posts a job → Maple Cleaning wins it → Ana shows up.**
Until these exist the flyer borrows the old cast, so Sara shows up as a man.
These three images fix that.

Save each file **in this folder** with the exact name. The flyer picks it up
by itself.

## Specs (every image)

- **Transparent background** (PNG). If your tool can't do that, use "on a plain
  bright green #00FF00 background" instead and I'll cut it out.
- **Portrait, about 1024 × 1536.** Full body, head to shoes, centred, nothing
  cut off, small empty margin all round.
- Make all three in **the same chat or seed**, so they look like one set.

## Style block — paste at the start of every prompt

> 3D cartoon character, modern stylized Pixar-like render, soft clay-like materials, smooth subsurface skin, big expressive eyes, friendly confident expression, clean bright studio lighting with soft rim light, vibrant colours — outfits in golden yellow (#FFCE48), coral (#FF6B8A), white, denim and teal, never purple, violet or navy — full body from head to shoes, standing, isolated on a transparent background, no text, no logo, no frame, high detail, 4k

**Avoid:** purple, violet or navy clothing, yellow as the main outfit colour
for Sara or Ana (the flyer background is yellow, they'd disappear), realistic
photo, uncanny face, extra fingers, cropped head or feet, text, watermark,
logo, busy background, shadows on the ground.
