# "What if…?" flyer — image and animation prompts

For `what-if.html`. The flyer already works without these. It's a late-night
lock screen filling with everyday worries, then one Dodorez notification sorts
them all out. These three characters add a face to it: a person who goes from
**worried to relieved**, and the dog whose walk started it all.

Save each file **in this folder** with the exact name. The flyer picks them up
by itself.

## Specs (every image)

- **Transparent background** (PNG). If your tool can't do that, use "on a plain
  bright green #00FF00 background" instead and I'll cut it out.
- **Portrait, about 1024 × 1536.** Full body or three-quarter, centred, with a
  small empty margin all round.
- Make all three in **the same chat or seed**, so they look like one set.

## Style block — paste at the start of every prompt

> 3D cartoon character, modern stylized Pixar-like render, soft clay-like materials, smooth subsurface skin, big expressive eyes, cinematic night-time lighting — cool blue moonlight from one side and a soft warm glow from a phone screen on the face, vibrant but moody colours, outfit in golden yellow (#FFCE48), coral (#FF6B8A), white, denim and teal, never purple or navy, isolated on a transparent background, no text, no logo, no frame, high detail, 4k

**Avoid:** purple or navy clothing, realistic photo, uncanny face, extra fingers,
cropped head or feet, text, watermark, logo, busy background, a bed or sofa that
fills the frame.

## The three images

| File | Prompt (after the style block) |
|---|---|
| `whatif-worried.png` | A woman in her early 30s with curly shoulder-length hair, in an oversized coral hoodie and grey sweatpants, sitting cross-legged on the floor late at night, holding her phone with both hands close to her face. The screen's glow lights her worried face: eyebrows raised, biting her lip, eyes wide, a tiny sweat drop. Three-quarter view, facing slightly left. |
| `whatif-relief.png` | **The same woman, same outfit and pose**, now relaxed: shoulders dropped, one hand on her chest, eyes softly closed, a big relieved smile, her phone held loosely in the other hand with a warm golden-yellow glow from the screen. Three-quarter view, facing slightly left. |
| `whatif-dog.png` | A friendly golden retriever named Bruno, sitting upright with a teal leash held in his mouth, tail mid-wag, big hopeful eyes looking up and to the right, ears perked. Full body. |

**Tip:** make `whatif-worried` first. Then, in the same chat: "Now the same
character, same outfit, same lighting, but relieved — " followed by the relief
prompt. That keeps her face identical, so the switch reads as one person.

## Animate them (optional)

The still images already move on the flyer: at the moment Dodorez arrives, the
worried picture fades into the relieved one, and the dog sways. For real motion,
turn the stills into short clips with an image-to-video tool (Runway, Kling,
Pika, Luma, Veo).

**Export:** 4–6 seconds, MP4, the character on a **plain bright green background
(#00FF00)**, with nothing else in frame and a camera that doesn't move. Send me
the MP4s; I'll cut out the green and make transparent loops, and an animated
post and story video.
